Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Out Of Line team (“we”, “us”, or “our”) expects to handle information in the Out Of Line Android game and on this website. Out Of Line is currently in development and is planned for release on Google Play. This policy will be reviewed and updated before the game becomes available.

Information collected by the game

The planned initial release is designed to work without an account and without requiring your name, email address, phone number, or other directly identifying information. Gameplay data such as scores or settings may be stored locally on your Android device so the game can remember your progress. We do not expect this local data to be transmitted to us.

Third-party services

At launch, the game may use services provided by Google Play, including Google Play’s distribution, security, and device compatibility services. Google may collect information according to its own policies and your device settings. If future versions add Google Play Games, advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or other third-party services, this policy will be updated to identify those services and explain the information they process before or when those features are introduced.

Website information

This website does not intentionally use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. GitHub Pages, the hosting provider, may process limited technical information when serving the site. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement for more information.

Children’s privacy

Out Of Line is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game will not request personal information from players as part of its core gameplay. Parents or guardians who have questions may contact us using the project’s official repository.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored gameplay data can generally be removed by clearing the game’s app data or uninstalling the game from your Android device. If a future feature allows us to receive personal information, we will explain how that information is retained and how you can request its deletion.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as development continues, when the game is published, or when our services change. The current version will always be posted on this page with its effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions about Out Of Line, please email support.smallstack@gmail.com. Please do not include sensitive personal information in your message.
